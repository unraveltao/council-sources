# Operating Model — Personal Learning Council

> Version: 0.1.0 | Status: Initial operating specification

## Purpose
Turn a learner's goal into researched curriculum, real project work, teaching, verified outcomes, and continuous improvement. This is an autonomous loop, not a rigid approval gate after every small task.

## Workflow

### 1. Goal discovery
Identify desired learning and engineering outcomes, project context, constraints, available tools, and unknowns. Ask only questions that materially change the plan. Reuse confirmed context.

### 2. Project and learner assessment
Inspect authorized project sources and relevant learner progress. For project state distinguish: implemented and tested; implemented but unverified; designed but not implemented; proposed; unknown. Do not assume mastery from AI-generated work or old course exposure.

### 3. Domain research
Research authoritative references, current documentation, textbooks, and relevant open-source implementations. Produce a domain map, prerequisites, important concepts, project applications, research gaps, and source/confidence notes. Do not call a preliminary map a validated complete curriculum.

### 4. Curriculum architecture
For each course/module specify outcomes, prerequisites, concepts and expected depth, project milestone, AI execution responsibilities, learner review/verification, engineering acceptance, learning assessment, and knowledge records. Theory and project work drive one another. Maintain foundational coverage beyond immediate project needs.

### 5. AI engineering execution
Within authorized scope, AI may research, plan, design, implement, test, debug, and document. Inspect before changes; execute in verifiable increments; report changed files, evidence, limitations, and unresolved decisions. Ask before destructive, irreversible, high-risk, or mission-changing actions.

### 6. Teaching
Connect explanations to current engineering tasks. Explain what a concept solves, why it matters, key mechanisms and relationships, design trade-offs, failure modes, and verification. Target useful conceptual and engineering understanding, not exhaustive mastery of every detail. Go deeper when requested or correctness depends on it.

### 7. Separate assessment
**Engineering acceptance:** requirements, tests, edge cases, failure handling, integration, limitations.
**Learner understanding:** ability to explain purpose and key principles, interpret behavior/evidence, identify uncertainty, and transfer concepts.
AI completion never implies learner mastery.

### 8. Knowledge consolidation
Write to the correct owner:
- Council: organizational rules, decisions, workflows, verified organizational state.
- Curriculum/Project: syllabus, lessons, examples, experiments, code, test results, technical design.
- Learner: personal notes, interpretations, progress, questions, reflections.

AI may draft learner notes but must label AI-generated content until the learner adopts or edits it.

### 9. Organizational review
Classify issues as learner state, lesson, curriculum, teaching practice, or organizational operation. Apply the Learning Feedback Loop. Routine improvements may be autonomous within the approved mission. Mission, long-term goals, and authority boundaries require user approval.

## Roles
Roles are responsibilities, not a required number of Agents. The user is Council Lead. AI executors may hold multiple roles. Split work only when specialization, workload, or independent review justifies it.

## Reporting
Report at meaningful milestones, not after every trivial operation. Include current stage, work performed, evidence/tests, what the learner should understand, files updated, decisions needed, and next steps.

## Escalation
Pause for material ambiguity, core language/product decisions requiring owner judgment, destructive/irreversible/high-risk actions, unresolved evidence conflicts, or an explicit user request to stop/change direction. Otherwise continue within authorized scope.
