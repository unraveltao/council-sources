# Personal Learning Council — Decision Register

> Version: 0.1.0 | Last updated: 2026-11-17

## D-001 — Comprehensive development goal
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Build a comprehensive capability spanning computer science foundations, AI engineering, collaborative technical judgment, creation with AI, and continuous research/learning.
- **Rationale:** The learner wants broad technical capability. Judgment is developed by reasoning with AI, comparing options, and evaluating evidence.

## D-002 — Project priorities
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Unravel Language is the primary engineering-learning project; AI Engineering is a parallel track; Lattice is paused.
- **Rationale:** Unravel provides a sustained path into programming-language and computer science concepts.

## D-003 — AI as primary executor
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** AI autonomously performs research and routine engineering within authorized scope. The learner focuses on understanding key principles, reviewing important choices, and verifying outcomes.
- **Rationale:** Use AI execution efficiently while preserving the learner's ability to understand and evaluate systems.

## D-004 — User-led organization
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** The user is Council Lead. No separate Council Director initially.
- **Rationale:** Initial workload does not justify another coordination role.

## D-005 — Knowledge ownership
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Separate organization knowledge, curriculum/project knowledge, and learner knowledge by ownership and write boundaries.
- **Rationale:** Organization standards, course materials, and personal understanding are different kinds of information.

## D-006 — Flexible personal knowledge storage
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Support Public, Private, and Hybrid storage. Do not require a separate private knowledge base.
- **Rationale:** Users choose their sharing model; actual privacy depends on storage permissions.

## D-007 — Autonomous improvement
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Autonomously improve courses, exercises, teaching methods, and routine workflows within mission. Mission, long-term goals, and fundamental authority boundaries require explicit user approval.
- **Rationale:** Improve from evidence without asking approval for every routine adjustment.

## D-008 — GitHub/local/GPT distribution
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** GitHub stores canonical Council Markdown; local Git is the working copy; GPT receives a consolidated Markdown snapshot through manual replacement. ZIP is a package/backup.
- **Rationale:** Low-complexity, auditable workflow without a custom sync API.

## D-009 — First curriculum
- **Status:** Confirmed
- **Decision:** Use Unravel Language design and development as the first complete curriculum and operating-model pilot.
- **Rationale:** It integrates computer science, software engineering, AI execution, teaching, and verification.
